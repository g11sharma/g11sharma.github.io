Lumen Plan – a mini EPM planning application

  • Business rules, what-if simulation and top-down spreading
  • Approval workflow, cell comments and audit trail
  • Variance analysis with price/volume/mix, and an ERP actuals import with month close

Lumen Consol – a financial consolidation simulator

A consolidation engine inspired by Oracle FCCS. A French parent consolidates its subsidiaries in the UK, the US (80% owned) and India at the half-year close.

What you can try

  • Currency translation (IAS 21) with editable rates and FX scenarios
  • Intercompany matching with tolerance, and fixing an invoice in transit
  • Automatic eliminations and FCCS-style journals (approval workflow, Entity and Contribution Input)
  • IFRS-aligned statements: goodwill at the closing rate, deferred tax on internal margins, non-controlling interests and comprehensive income

Lumen Rollout – an EPM deployment project kit

An interactive case study of the project that rolls out Oracle EPM Planning and FCCS at a fictional French group, shown in the middle of user acceptance testing.

Paris Market AI – an AI that reads the stock market every morning

Every weekday, a local AI (Mistral) reads French financial and world news, and a machine-learning model ranks the most traded stocks on Euronext Paris. Unlike the demos above, it runs on real market data and updates itself every morning.

What you can try

  • Today's market brief and world context (wars, interest rates, energy) written by the AI, with the sectors it affects
  • A daily BUY / HOLD / SELL ranking of every stock, with the facts behind each signal
  • Long-term and risk profiles (volatility, beta, worst drop) and a risk vs return map
  • An honest track record: a backtest with trading costs, live paper trading on €100,000 of virtual money, and the AI's answers to the day's questions
